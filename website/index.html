Open source · Privacy-first email

Why ICMail

Your mailbox, on your terms

No lock-in, no harvesting, no compromise. ICMail is built around a simple idea — your email is yours.

Privacy by design

Block external images, control read receipts, and connect directly to your provider. Your data never detours through us.

All your accounts, unified

Add multiple accounts and ICMail builds a unified inbox, sent and trash automatically — every message in one place.

Open & extensible

Licensed under GPL-3.0 and built with Flutter. Inspect it, improve it, and extend it for your company or your provider.

Features

Everything a grown-up mail app should do

From WYSIWYG composing to universal search, ICMail covers the daily essentials and the power-user extras.

WYSIWYG compose

Write, reply and forward with a rich editor, drafts, GIFs and stickers via Giphy.

Universal search

Search across messages and accounts, then bulk-act on the results.

In-app attachments

View photos, PDFs, audio and video right inside the app, then share or forward them.

Configurable swipes

Swipe to delete or mark read/unread — and map the gestures to your liking.

One-tap unsubscribe

Cut through newsletter clutter and unsubscribe without leaving the app.

Threads & aliases

Full thread view where supported, plus alias and "+ address" handling.

Archive, junk & cleanup

Archive mail, flag junk/not-junk and empty trash or junk in one move.

Themes & languages

Light, dark and custom color schemes. Localized in English and German.
